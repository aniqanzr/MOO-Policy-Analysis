The Certificate of Entitlement (COE) quota for August 2023 to October 2023 will be 11,019 . Details of the quota for each COE category are in Annex A . Bidding under this quota will start on 7 August 2023. 2. The COE quota consists of the following components: a. 25% of the replacement COEs from vehicles deregistered in the twelve-month period from July 2022 to June 2023; b. Provision for 0.25% per annum growth for Category C based on the Category C vehicle population as at 31 December 2022; and c. Adjustments for changes in the taxi population, expired COEs, Early Turnover Scheme for commercial vehicles and redistribution from guaranteed deregistrations for Category A and Category B. 3. The next quota announcement for the bidding period of November 2023 to January 2024 will be made in October 2023.
