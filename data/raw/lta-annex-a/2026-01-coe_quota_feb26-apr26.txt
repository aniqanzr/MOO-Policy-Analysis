The Certificate of Entitlement (COE) quota for February 2026 to April 2026 will be 18,824 . Details of the quota for each COE category are in Annex A . 2. The February 2026 to April 2026 COE quota consists of the following components: a. 25% of the replacement COEs from vehicles deregistered in the twelve-month period from January 2025 to December 2025; b. Provision for 0.25% per annum growth for Category C based on the Category C vehicle population as at 31 December 2025; and c. Adjustments for changes in the taxi population, expired Temporary COEs, Early Turnover Scheme for commercial vehicles, redistribution from guaranteed deregistrations for Category A, Category B and Category D and injection of additional COEs. 3. Overall, the COE quota for February 2026 to April 2026 is 1% lower than the previous quarter (November 2025 to January 2026). 4. Bidding under this quota will start on 2 February 2026. Bidding exercises start on the first and third Monday of each month. There will be a longer interval of three weeks between the 2 nd bidding exercise of March 2026 and the 1 st bidding exercise of April 2026. 5. The next quota announcement for the bidding period of May 2026 to July 2026 will be made in April 2026.
